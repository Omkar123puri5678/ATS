# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming, harassment-free space for everyone, whatever their age,
body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- Being kind, patient and respectful
- Respecting different opinions and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning our mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what's best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Sexualized language or imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insults, or personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or email, without permission
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when
someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer,
[@Omkar123puri5678](https://github.com/Omkar123puri5678). All reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break
this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors who do.
